## 1️⃣ INTEGRATION OF CULTS — "**DAR**"
- **D**isseminate: Puranic texts in **simple Sanskrit** for **women & Shudras**
- **A**ccept + **R**ework: local deities → wives of gods (**Lakshmi/Parvati**)
- ⭐ Example: **PURI — Jagannatha** ("lord of the world") = form of **VISHNU**, 12th century, with **Subhadra + Balabhadra**
- **Tantric**: open to **women + men**, ignored **caste/class** in rituals; influenced **Shaivism + Buddhism**
- **Conflict corner:** Vedic gods **A-I-S (Agni, Indra, Soma)** faded; Vedas still revered; Vedic vs Tantric tension; devotees projected own deity supreme; tension with **Buddhism/Jainism**

**BLURT:** Two processes? → DAR. Puri example? → Jagannatha-Vishnu-12th c. Faded Vedic trio? → AIS.

## 2️⃣ ALVARS & NAYANARS (c. 6th c., Tamil Nadu) — "**A-V, N-S**"
- **Alvars = Vishnu** ("immersed") • **Nayanars = Shiva** • travelled + **sang TAMIL hymns** → shrines
- Castes: **artisan, cultivator, outcaste**; protest against **Buddhism & Jainism**; later merged into Brahmanical order
- **Anthologies:**

| Text | Who | Fact |
|---|---|---|
| **Nalayira Divyaprabandham** | 12 Alvars | "Four Thousand Compositions" = **TAMIL VEDA**, by 10th c. |
| **Tevaram** | Appar, Sambandar, Sundarar | compiled **10th c. on basis of MUSIC** |
- **Women:** **ANDAL** (Alvar, beloved/bride of Vishnu, sung today) • **KARAIKKAL AMMAIYAR** (Nayanar, extreme asceticism, "female **PEY** demoness", Shiva-dancer's forest **Alankatu**) → both **challenged patriarchy**, did NOT become nuns
- **Sources:** Tondaradippodi (Brahmana Alvar): outcaste devotees > **chaturvedins** • Appar: "rogues who quote law books… gotra-kula useless"
- **State link:** **Pallavas & Pandyas** (6th–9th c.) → **CHOLAS (9th–13th)**: land grants + temples; Shiva temples at **C-T-G (Chidambaram, Thanjavur, Gangaikondacholapuram)**; bronze Nataraja era; saints revered by **VELLALA peasants**; kings used saints' popularity for **divine legitimacy**

**BLURT:** Alvar god? → Vishnu. Tamil Veda? → Nalayira Divyaprabandham. Tevaram compiled on? → Music. Chola temples? → CTG.

## 3️⃣ VIRASHAIVA / LINGAYAT (12th c., Karnataka) — "**B-B-W**"
- **Basavanna** (Brahmana minister) + Allama Prabhu + Akkamahadevi • texts = **VACHANAS in KANNADA**
- Worshipped **Shiva**; wore **linga**; revered **jangama** (wandering monk)
- **Rejected: Vedas, rebirth, caste, purity-pollution** → therefore **BURIED their dead** ✝️
- Encouraged **post-puberty marriage + widow REMARRIAGE**
- ⭐ **Vachana extract:** milk on **stone serpent** / kill real one; feed **stone image** / shoo hungry **servant of god** → mocks **empty ritual**, praises human service

**BLURT:** Sacred text? → Vachanas (Kannada). Burial because? → rejected rebirth. Basavanna's method? → paradox/contrast.

## 4️⃣ NORTH INDIA (why bhakti came late) — "**R-N-T**"
- **R**ajput states → **B**rahmanas strong → no direct challenge → **no Alvar-type bhakti till 14th c.**
- **N**aths, Jogis, **S**iddhas: from **artisan/weaver** groups; **anti-Veda**; spoke **people's languages**; BUT **no elite support**
- **T**urks arrived → Delhi Sultanate (13th c.) → Rajput-Brahmana power broke → **SUFIS entered** (§6-7)

**BLURT:** Who filled the gap? → Naths-Jogis-Siddhas. Their background? → Artisan/weavers. Trigger for Sufis? → Turkish conquest.

## 5️⃣ ISLAMIC TRADITIONS — "**711 → Sultanate → Mughal**"
- **711:** **Muhammad Qasim** conquered **SIND** (Caliph's domain) • 13th c.: **Delhi Sultanate** • 16th: **Mughals**
- **Ulama** = scholars; **SHARI'A** = law from **Q-H-Q-I** (Qur'an, Hadis, Qiyas=analogy, Ijma=consensus)
- **ZIMMI** = "protected" (Jews/Christians… extended to **Hindus**) → paid **JIZYA** → got protection
- **Flexible rulers:** land grants + tax exemptions to **Hindu, Jaina, Zoroastrian, Christian, Jewish** institutions (by **Akbar AND Aurangzeb**)
  - ⭐ Akbar **FARMAN 1598**: church for **padris (Jesus Society)** at **Khambat (Gujarat)**
  - ⭐ Aurangzeb **letter 1661-62**: gift (cloth + **₹25**) to **Shiv Murat Guru Anand Nath** (Jogi)
- **Popular Islam:** **KHOJAHS** (Ismaili Shia) → **GINAN** devotional poems (from Sanskrit *jnana*) in regional languages, **oral via pirs**, written in **KHOJAKI script** (from **landa** = clipped merchant script, 17th c.)
- **Malabar (Kerala):** Arab traders' descendants adopted **Malayalam + MATRILINY + matrilocal residence**
- **Mosque:** universal = **Mecca orientation, MIHRAB (niche), MINBAR (pulpit)**; local = roofs/materials (Kerala mosque c. 13th c., shikhara-like roof)
- **Names (8th–14th c. Sanskrit sources):** Muslim label NOT used → **TURUSHKA (Turks), TAJIKA (Tajik), PARASHIKA (Persia)**

**BLURT:** Shari'a 4 sources? → QHQI. Zimmi tax? → Jizya. Khojah script? → Khojaki from landa. Mosque universals? → Mihrab-Minbar-Mecca.

## 6️⃣ SUFI BASICS — "**SPM → MKK**"
- Master = **SHAIKH / PIR / MURSHID** • disciple = **MURID** • successor = **KHALIFA** • hospice = **KHANQAH**
- **SILSILA** = "chain" → unbroken link master→disciple→**PROPHET MUHAMMAD**; crystallised **12th century**
- Initiation: **oath of allegiance + patched garment + shaved hair**
- Death → tomb = **DARGAH** (Persian: "court") • **WALI/AULIYA** (friend of God) • **BARAKAT** (grace) • **KARAMAT** (miracles)
- Names: **Chishti** (town Chisht), **Qadiri** (Jilani), **Suhrawardi**, **Naqshbandi** (Baha'uddin Naqshband)
- **Be-shari'a** mystics outside khanqah = **QALANDARS** (mendicant, celibate, anti-ritual)

**BLURT:** Silsila means? → Chain to Prophet. Dargah literal? → Court. Outside-khanqah mystics? → Qalandars.

- ⭐ **Charkhanama** (spinning song): **zikr-i jali / qalbi / aini**, 24,000 breaths as gift to **pir**

### (e) Sufis & State — "**A-I-L**"

- Kings wanted **L**egitimation: Sultanate **resisted ulama's shari'a state law** (non-Muslim majority!) → took authority from **sufis (direct from God)** • believed **auliya could intercede with Allah** • kings wanted **tombs near dargahs**

## 8️⃣ SAINT-POETS — "**K-N-M**"
### KABIR (c. 14th–15th c.)
- **Julaha (weaver)**, disciple? → **Ramananda** (legend) • attacked **BOTH** religions' externals (idols/pilgrimage/baths + circumcision/rules) → mocked **Brahmanas & mullahs**
- Formless divine: **ALAKH, NIRANJAN** • style: **ULTABANSI** (upside-down paradox)
- **3 compilations — "B-G-A":** **B**ijak → **Kabirpanth** (Varanasi/UP) • **G**ranthavali → **Dadupanth** (Rajasthan) • **A**di Granth Sahib (Punjab)

### BABA GURU NANAK (1469–1539)
- Born **Nankana Sahib, near RAVI** (Muslim-majority Punjab) • **Hindu merchant family** • trained **ACCOUNTANT**, studied **PERSIAN** • married young, wandered with sufis-bhaktas
- **NIRGUNA bhakti** • rejected: sacrifices, ritual baths, idol worship, austerities, **scriptures of BOTH** • God = **"RAB", no gender/form**
- Practice: repeat **Divine Name**; hymns = **SHABAD in PUNJABI**, sung in **ragas**, **MARDANA on RABAB**
- Community: **SANGAT** (congregational worship) → successor **ANGAD** → ~200 years of Gurus
- **GURU ARJAN (5th):** compiled **ADI GRANTH SAHIB** = Nanak + 4 successors + **Farid, Ravidas, Kabir**; hymns = **GURBANI**
- **GURU GOBIND SINGH (10th, late 17th c.):** added **Tegh Bahadur's** hymns → **GURU GRANTH SAHIB**; founded **KHALSA PANTH** ("army of the pure") — 5 symbols: **uncut hair, dagger, shorts, comb, steel bangle** → socio-religious + military force

### MIRABAI (c. 15th–16th c.)
- **Rajput princess, MERTA (Marwar)** • married **against her wishes** • defied husband & norms
- **GURU = RAIDAS** (leather-worker, "untouchable") → **direct challenge to high-caste + patriarchy**
- Devotion to **KRISHNA**; bhajans transmitted **ORALLY**; loved by common folk (Rajasthan/Gujarat memory)

### 📦 Bonus: SHANKARADEVA (late 15th c., Assam)
- **Bhagavati dharma** (Gita + Bhagavata Purana) • **NAM KIRTAN** • **SATRA** (monastery) • **NAAM GHAR** (prayer hall)

# 🟡 THEME 7: AN IMPERIAL CAPITAL — VIJAYANAGARA (6 Marks)

## 🎬 THE WHOLE CHAPTER IN 5 LINES

## 1️⃣ DISCOVERY — "**1754-1800-1856**"
- **Colin Mackenzie** (b. **1754**) → **1800: FIRST survey map of Hampi ruins** → later **FIRST SURVEYOR GENERAL OF India**
- Sources: **local informants + oral traditions** • **1856: Alexander Greenlaw** — first **photographs** (calotypes) → epigraphists → archaeologists
- Historical sources: **inscriptions** (kings + nayakas: donations, events) + **travellers**:

## 2️⃣ KINGS & TRADERS — "**G-A-N**"

- Titles: **GAJAPATI** = lord of **elephants** (Orissa lineage) • **ASHVAPATI** = lord of **horses** (Deccan Sultans) • **NARAPATI** = lord of **men** (the Rayas)

## 4️⃣ AMARA-NAYAKA SYSTEM — "**T-R-T-T**" ⭐ (best 4-marker)
Nayakas = military chiefs (**Telugu/Kannada**), held forts + troops; some conquered areas, some royal appointees.
1. **T**axes: collected from peasants/traders/artisans in their **amara**
2. **R**etain: part of revenue for **personal use + maintaining horses/troops/elephants**
3. **T**ribute: **annual tribute to king**
4. **T**roops: supplied **military contingents** on demand
- ≈ **IQTA system** of Delhi Sultanate (scholars' parallel)
- Post-1565: nayakas became **independent kingdoms — Mysore, Keladi (Ikkeri), Madurai, Tanjavur**

**BLURT:** 4 duties? → TRTT. Parallel system? → Iqta. New kingdoms? → Mysore-Keladi-Madurai-Tanjavur.

## 5️⃣ THE CITY — "**7-F-W**"

- **Water — "H-K-A-S":** **H**iriya canal (from **dam on Tungabhadra**) • **K**amalapuram tank (15th c: irrigation + royal centre) • **A**queducts → royal centre • **S**tepped tank (sacred centre)
- Roads radiated from **royal centre**; gateways controlled entry

**BLURT:** Why farms inside forts? → Siege survival + revenue protection. Hiriya canal source? → Dam on Tungabhadra.

## 6️⃣ ROYAL CENTRE — "**D-L-H**"
- **"King's palace"** = largest complex (identified by **PAES**); fragile materials → poorly preserved
### MAHANAVAMI DIBBA ⭐ SOURCE BOX
- Massive platform, **highest point • base ~11,000 sq. ft • height 40 ft** • **wooden superstructure** • **relief carvings** on base
- **Mahanavami** = "great ninth day" of **10-day festival** = **DUSEHRA (north) / DURGA PUJA (Bengal) / NAVARATRI (peninsular)** — Sept–Oct
- Ceremonies: king **inspected army**, received **tribute & gifts from nayakas**, watched **processions, dances, wrestling**, feasted guests → **wealth & power display + ritual loyalty**
- **"HOUSE OF VICTORY"** (Paes) = dibba + audience hall: *"two platforms one above the other… room made of cloth… idol has a shrine… dais… THRONE OF STATE (crown + royal anklet)"*
- → **king's durbar + idol-shrine = royal power fused with ritual**
### Other buildings

- **"Elephant stables"** nearby (identification questioned)
- **HAZARA RAMA TEMPLE:** **king & family ONLY**; central idols **missing**; walls carry **RAMAYANA panels** (+ elephants, horses, dancers)
- **Nayakas continued** the tradition (e.g., **audience hall, Madurai** — arches)

**BLURT:** Dibba digits? → 11,000-40. House of Victory =? → Dibba + audience hall (Paes). Lotus Mahal name by? → British; purpose? → council chamber (Mackenzie). Hazara Rama =? → Royal family temple, Ramayana walls.
